## Post 1

Link- https://www.linkedin.com/posts/coldemailwizard_everything-in-your-business-operates-on-a-activity-7450202730859712512-8Bgb

Everything in your business operates on a "contingency system." Let's say you're doing cold email to get clients. Here's an example of the steps involved:
 
1) Buying the domains
2) Setting up the inboxes
3) Building the lead lists
4) Connecting the inboxes to a sending tool
5) Writing scripts
6) Setting up the campaigns
7) Managing the replies
8) Taking the sales calls
 
To do one step, you must have already done the previous step. This is what contingency means.
 
Every single thing you can possibly do in your business all works on a contingency system like this.
 
If you were running ads, that has its own system. Sales calls themselves have their own system. Fulfilling for clients has a similar system.
 
The reason a business stalls is because one of the steps stops.
 
That's it.
 
A step can be completed 1 of 4 ways:
 
1) You do it
2) Somebody else does it
3) Automation
4) AI (autonomous cognition)
 
You obviously want to automate as much as you can. But that's not always possible.
 
The entirety of your business operates on a system of systems. Here's what that means:
 
In order for you to make money:
 
1) You must fulfill for a client
2) But in order to fulfill for a client, you must have closed the client in your sales process
3) But in order to have closed a client in your sales process, you must have generated them as a lead in your marketing process
 
So Marketing, Sales, and Fulfillment are THEMSELVES a contingency system. And each one if comprised of their own individual contingency systems.
 
So, basically, your entire business could be mapped out as dozens of different boxes of shit that needs to be done every day. And the only task you ever need to do is one of those boxes.
 
Where people fail in their business is that they fail to get all the boxes done.
 
Yes, there might be 30-50 different things you need to be doing in your business. And a lot of times, you don't want to do 4-5 of those things because you don't like them. So you stop doing them. And, therefore, your business COMPLETELY halts, because the entire system is contingent upon those things being done.
 
This is why AI has become such an important development. Previously, to do anything that required cognition, you needed a human. But you now have access to a computer that can do human level thinking for a couple cents. This makes scaling way easier and less expensive.
 
This is a very important concept to grasp.

## Insight
- A business is a chain of dependent systems—progress only flows when every step is completed. Bottlenecks, not effort, cause failure. Growth comes from identifying and clearing constraints.

## Post 2

Link- https://www.linkedin.com/posts/coldemailwizard_my-student-was-struggling-to-sign-clients-activity-7449441289479122944-_FnU

My student was struggling to sign clients with cold email. I asked her to send me her script and the lead list she was sending emails to. I then dumped her info into Claude and managed to fix her campaign in 10 seconds.

It turns out she was emailing people that are not relevant decision makers. 

People like property managers, regional managers, assistant managers, etc. 

Nobody was responding because no relevant people were even getting the emails in the first place.
 
She immediately gets a new list, instead with Founders/Owners. 3 days later, she sends me a message that it's now working and they're getting leads/calls booked.
 
Something similar happened with another student of mine.

They were selling an Amazon FBA agency offer and he was emailing ecommerce brands.

He told me his response rate was terrible and he thinks he needs a new offer.

So I had him open up the lead lists he was sending to and pick 10 random leads from the list.

We opened up their website live on my coaching call.

0/10 he opened were actually ecommerce brands.
 
So the entire list he was sending to wasn't relevant.

Which led to him incorrectly thinking and freaking out that he needs to change his entire business' offer.
 
These stories are exemplifying two things:
 
1) Stop making obvious mistakes. Of course you're not going to get responses if you're sending your emails to the wrong people
2) Use AI to help you. AI is your hyper intelligent assistant that can do anything
 
All I had to do was upload the lead lists, the scripts, and all the responses to an LLM. Here's the exact prompt I gave it:
 
"All of these are documents in regards to a cold email campaign that is not converting. Go through the leads and make sure they're relevant, then also all of the other docs including the scripts we're sending, and try to help me determine what the problem is and why the cold emails are not converting. Just give me a straightforward solution of what is wrong with the campaign and why it's not converting"
 
Then I got an answer that solved the problem in 10 seconds.
 
You need to be somebody who knows HOW to figure out problems.
 
I had no idea what the issue with the cold email campaign was. It would probably take me hours to go through everything and spend a lot of time thinking about it. But instead of doing that, I can just pay 1.2 cents to an AI system to analyze and diagnose it for me in 10 seconds.
 
This same process could apply for analyzing and diagnosing performance problems of:

Video Sales Letters
Ad Scripts
Sales Pages
Dream 100 Outreach Messages
YouTube Videos
Short Form Videos
Email Sequences

If something isn't performing like you want, just throw ALL the data you have in. Give it context and explain what it's looking at and what your issue is. It will analyze everything and tell you what's most probably wrong. Voila - your issues are fixed.

Here are 6 plays that convert at 3-4x standard outbound your GTM team should likely be running but probably aren't yet at scale:

1. The "Boomerang" Play

Track former employees of your own company who have moved into roles at prospect or customer accounts. Former employees understand your product deeply, often carry positive sentiment, and can be powerful internal champions or at minimum a warm first conversation.

Benefit:
Converts your own company's alumni network into a distributed sales force of informed, credible internal advocates at target accounts.

What you need: Contact Data | Employment History | Job Change Alerts


2. The "Tech Stack Transition" Play

Identify companies that recently removed a technology from their stack AND are showing intent signals on a replacement category AND have posted jobs requiring skills in that new category. All three signals together confirm an active, in-progress technology transition.

Benefit:
Catches companies mid-transition before they have selected a new vendor, putting your reps into an active evaluation at the earliest possible stage.

What you need: Technographics | Intent | Job Postings


3. The "Competitive Migration" Play

Layer competitor technographic install + negative review signals + intent surge on your category + recent leadership change. Each signal alone is interesting. Together they paint a picture of an account that is unhappy, actively researching alternatives, and has new leadership with a mandate to fix it.

Benefit:
Surfaces the highest-probability competitive displacement targets with surgical precision, allowing reps to prioritize the accounts most likely to switch right now.

What you need: Technographics | Intent | Scoops

4. The "Org Chart Stress" Play

Identify accounts experiencing significant org chart disruption – multiple leadership changes, a wave of departures in a specific department, or a rapid restructuring – combined with intent signals suggesting the disruption is creating operational pain.

Benefit:
Surfaces accounts where internal disruption is actively creating the pain your solution solves, enabling reps to show up as a stabilizing force at exactly the right moment.

What you need: Scoops | Contact Data | Org Charts | Intent


5. The "Ghost Account" Reactivation

Identify prospects who previously engaged heavily – attended a webinar, requested a demo, entered a trial – and then went completely dark. Cross-reference with signals to understand why they went dark and identify the right moment to re-engage.

Benefit:
Reactivates a warm audience that already knows your product, dramatically reducing the cost and effort of re-engagement compared to net new prospecting.

What you need: Intent | WebSights | Engagement | Scoops

6. Conference + Intent + Title Signals Play

Identify individuals attending an upcoming conference who also match your ideal buyer title AND whose company is showing active intent signals on your solution category. This is a prioritized, pre-qualified meeting list for the event.

Benefit: 
Transforms a generic conference attendee list into a precision-targeted meeting schedule, ensuring every on-site conversation is with a pre-qualified, actively interested buyer.

What you need: Events Data | Intent | Contact Data

## Insight
- Highest-performing outbound comes from signal stacking, not volume. Combining multiple intent, timing, and context signals surfaces in-market buyers, letting reps engage earlier, warmer, and with far higher conversion probability.

This play is GOLD!

It’s historically converted at 5x standard outbound rates.

Every single Rev Ops, GTM Engineer or GTM leader should be building out an agent that is pushing this data to your reps in real time.

Remember… outbound is a game of precision.

Timing is everything.

Especially if you are selling into larger accounts.

If you’ve sold to enterprise, you know this all to well.

It’s EXTREMELY difficult to rip out another vendor if there is a contract in place.

That’s why this play is fantastic.

I built it out in GTM Studio and it tells you:

1. What vendor somebody might have in place

2. When their contract renewal data is likely coming up

From here, we can:

1. Cross reference this against other signals like call recording transcripts, web visits etc.

2. Build out initial messaging so the rep does as little non-revenue generating work as possible

3. Alert the rep at the perfect time

Anybody running this play?? 

## Insight
- Winning enterprise outbound hinges on timing + contract awareness. Selling during renewal windows avoids displacement friction. Layering signals and automating alerts turns outbound into precise, high-probability engagement instead of guesswork.

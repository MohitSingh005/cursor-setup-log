This is the framework I use to consistently build  pipeline from cold emails.

I try to tell a story and let each email in my sequence build on the next.

Here's an example:

Email #1:

Hey {{first-name}} - [mention a buying trigger].

I'd imagine that means your neck deep dealing with [problem statement] because of [the buying trigger].

If it sounds relevant, I can share how [similar company or someone in similar role] tackled that.

Open to seeing it?
___

The first email is intentionally vague, because that piques curiosity.

We want our buyer wondering how do we solve the problem. 

First, show them you've done your homework about their world.

Then, explain the problem clearer than they can in the second line.

Finally, in the third line/CTA, create curiosity with the goal of getting a conversation started.

The Chrome extension I use for relevant triggers is Evabot AI for Sales Outbound 2.0. It researches accounts for me so I have relevant buying signs.

Email #2: same thread

For more context on [problem statement], {{first-name}}, most folks don't have the [time/resources/budget] so when [buying trigger] occurs they get stretched.

Here’s a visual to explain how others avoided this:

[Insert a screenshot of what your product does to solve this problem]

Think this could help?
___

In the second email, we're explaining how we solve the problem with a visual.

Visuals are digested in magnitudes of order faster than words alone.

Email #3: same thread

{{first-name}} - it's common for [problem 1 & problem 2] to be top of mind given [buying trigger]. Others have told me this has been helpful: [insert helpful resource or thought leadership piece]

Mind if I share a couple more resources?
___

The third email is now building upon the last two.

We're showing the buyer they're not alone in dealing with their problems and there might be some creative ways they've not considered on how to handle them.

The resource you share ideally comes from a 3rd party to avoid being sales-y with marketing mumbo jumbo.

Plus a 3rd party resource builds credibility.

A week later, repeat with a new thread in a new email chain a different problem statement.

Have 2-3 laser focused, clear problem statements and you've got a full sequence to run.


## Insight
- progressive storytelling builds curiosity and trust across emails. Lead with triggers, deepen problem clarity, then show proof. However, vagueness risks low replies—balance curiosity with clear value and specificity.

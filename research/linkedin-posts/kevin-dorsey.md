The dirty secret of SaaS is that most of it only works if you're already good.

Salesforce doesn't make you a better sales leader. It makes a good sales leader faster.

HubSpot doesn't make you a better marketer. It makes a good marketer scalable.

Clay doesn't make you a GTM engineer. It gives an existing GTM engineer a bigger hammer.

Every piece of software you've bought in the last 15 years has been a force multiplier on skill you already had. 

Zero skill in = zero skill out. The tool was a mirror.

That's why most SaaS implementations fail. The company bought the tool hoping it would generate the skill. It never does.

Just spent an hour with Amos Bar-Joseph at Swan AI and we landed on a new term of where things are going. 

We're finally entering the era of Software WITH a Skill.

Open Swan. Don't see an empty workflow builder. Don't see a "connect your nodes" tutorial. See an actual play already loaded based on actual GTM expert skills. 

Executive air cover on stalled deals. Closed-lost re-engagement. Website de-anon into Slack. 

The triggers are defined. The sequence is defined. The escalation logic is defined.

You're not being asked to be a GTM engineer. 

You're being asked to be a GTM LEADER — define the thresholds, pick the execs, approve the motion. Five decisions and it's running.

Amos framed it as Neo in the Matrix. Load the disc. Know Kung Fu instantly. Swan is Neo, the skills are the disc.

What this means for CROs:

→ The moat stops being your tech stack. It starts being how clearly you can articulate great sales.

→ The $250K GTM engineer hire stops being the gating item.

→ Your 15 years of experience finally becomes downloadable.

Pre-Built workflows, that you can literally just click and set up, built with skills already and waiting for you to add your own.

Login and say 'I want a workflow that will go through all my closed lost deals from the last 18 months that never got back to us and create an exec to exec outreach system'

DONE - Money that was just sitting out there is now coming back in. 

The ones who can describe their motion in decisions — not vibes — are about to lap the ones who can only describe it in war stories.

Your experience is the product now. The software is just the delivery mechanism.

Getswan.com if you want to see what this looks like in the wild.

Software with a Skill

That's the NEW system.

## Insight
- Traditional SaaS amplifies existing skill; it doesn’t create it. The shift is toward embedded expertise—tools encoding best practices into workflows, turning experience into scalable systems and lowering dependency on specialized talent.

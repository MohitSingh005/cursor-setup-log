# AI-Driven Cold Outreach Playbook for B2B SaaS

## Step 1: Define ICP
- Identify target industry, role, company size
- Focus on high-probability buyers

---

## Step 2: Research Prospects
- Use LinkedIn and company websites
- Identify triggers (hiring, growth, funding)

---

## Step 3: Personalization
- Reference relevant context
- Focus on their problem, not your product

---

## Step 4: Email Structure

1. Hook (relevant opener)
2. Context (why them)
3. Value (problem you solve)
4. CTA (simple question)

---

## Step 5: Example Email

Subject: Quick question

Hi [Name],

Saw your team is scaling outbound.

Curious — are you currently facing challenges with reply rates?

We’ve helped similar teams improve responses by 2–3x.

Worth a quick chat?

---

## Step 6: Follow-up Strategy

- Follow-up after 2–3 days
- Keep it short
- Add new value

---

## Step 7: AI Integration

- Use AI for:
  - Personalization
  - Message generation
  - Prospect research

---

## Step 8: Optimization

- Track open and reply rates
- Test subject lines
- Iterate weekly
